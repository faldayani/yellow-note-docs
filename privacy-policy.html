Privacy Policy

App: Yellow Note  ·  Last updated: May 2026

Yellow Note is a personal note-taking app developed by Faisal Aldayani. This policy explains what data the app collects and how it is handled.

Data We Collect

Yellow Note does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All notes, to-do lists, and settings are stored exclusively on your device.

Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device only:

  • Notes and to-do lists you create (text, titles, tags, colors, target dates)
  • App preferences (theme, sort order, color labels)
  • Backup files created by the in-app backup feature (stored in your device's Downloads folder)

None of this data leaves your device.

Internet Access

The app requests internet permission for potential future features. It does not currently make any network requests and does not communicate with any server.

Backup & Restore

The optional backup feature exports an encrypted file to your device's local storage. This file never leaves your device unless you choose to share or move it manually.

Third-Party Services

Yellow Note does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date.
